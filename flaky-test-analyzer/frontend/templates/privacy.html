PRIVACY

How analysis data is handled

Uploaded JUnit and Playwright trace files are processed to produce your analysis. Uploaded artifacts are not intentionally retained after processing; temporary upload handling follows the application's cleanup behavior.

When enabled, privacy-first analytics stores minimal anonymous usage events and optional feedback. It does not intentionally store uploaded filenames, test or class names, diagnostic contents, stack traces, selectors, trace URLs, source code, network contents, credentials, or IP addresses.

A random first-party anonymous session identifier connects product events within one browser for a configurable period. It does not directly identify a person and analytics session counts do not prove unique people.

AI analysis is optional. When you enable it, sanitized diagnostic evidence may be sent to the configured AI provider. Sanitization is best-effort, so review artifacts before using AI.

Only upload artifacts that you are authorized to analyze, and avoid including secrets or unnecessary sensitive information.
